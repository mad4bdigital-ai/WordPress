## Comparison interpretation

Existing MAD4B source references identify a foundation to extend, not a claim that the listed parity is missing or already complete. GA4/SEO/commerce/forms breadth, human-facing Undo, granular consent and AI application layers need independent implementation and acceptance.

AI Engine RAG, AI Forms and realtime-audio entries are explicitly Pro claims in this package. miniOrange DLP/prompt-injection/anomaly/reputation entries remain claimed/unverified. No static observation can become live certification.

Competitor ideas are expressed through provider-neutral contracts. Original archives remain inert references outside runtime/package paths; no competitor is installed or enabled. Tool-count inflation, generic full-access scopes and unrestricted shell/PHP/raw SQL are not parity goals.
