# Contract — Adaptive Capability Fabric v2 Runtime Safety

Contract: `mad4b.adaptive-capability-fabric-v2-runtime.v1`.
This is optional future work, not runtime implementation or release authorization.

## Generation and proposal records

RuntimeGraphSnapshot binds provider/component/artifact/site/runtime generation,
operation/input/output schemas, registered routes/symbol provenance, resource
scope, preconditions, effect/reversal observations, sensitivity/cost, dependencies
and evidence. Discovery observes registered descriptors or statically inspected
approved artifacts; it does not include/activate unknown PHP, trigger autoload
effects, call arbitrary endpoints, dump option secrets or expose raw SQL.

SemanticDiff identifies additions/removals/schema/effect/permission changes and
affected dependencies. PolicyProposal records feature inputs, classifier/policy
revision, contradictions, uncertainty, suggested classification and required
proof. Names, annotations, methods, schema and confidence are hints, not authority
or behavioral evidence. Unknown side effects remain UNCLASSIFIED_FAIL_CLOSED.

Existing approved recipes may classify a genuinely non-secret, deterministic,
zero-effect read after provenance and conformance. New writes/high-risk semantics,
authority widening, sensitive data access and effect uncertainty require governed
review. Discovery, proposal or compatible version never grants or mounts a tool.

## Declarative manifests and synthesis

Manifest fields: identity/version/signature, exact artifact/capability/generation,
reviewed executor strategy ID, typed arguments/result schema, object scope,
preconditions, effect/cost/egress/secret policy, readback and rollback recipe IDs.
Only registered typed executors can interpret these records. No arbitrary
function/class/symbol/URL lookup, eval, generated PHP, generic shell/HTTP/SQL,
script hooks or opaque callback strings. Unsupported strategy requires reviewed
implementation; a signed manifest cannot bypass interpreter policy.

ContentExperienceCandidate is schema-derived, with no business/CPT names in core.
OperationGraph composes content/meta/taxonomy/media/SEO/builder/translation/
publication primitives and resolves available providers through current contracts.
Before execution, pin DAG/schema/provider/input/object/effect/authority/approval
digests and fencing/idempotency. A provider replacement or semantic replan creates
a new plan and invalidates affected approval; it cannot silently switch executors.

## Shadow and reversible canary

ShadowRead compares certified/candidate object identity, fields, semantic values,
error behavior, privacy and latency under configured workload/sample thresholds.
Candidate output is discarded from the active response. No shadow of an uncertain
effect or secret-access path; duplicated external read charges require admitted
cost/budget authority. Inadequate, stale or correlated samples cannot promote.

ReversibleCanary uses current pre-existing Staging authority, an approved recipe,
disposable isolated target, bounded effects/cost, native pre-state, once-only
candidate write, exact readback, certified rollback and exact restoration/cleanup.
Financial/email/webhook or other irreversible effects must be excluded or have
their own explicitly approved effect contract; they cannot inherit reversible
catalog certification. Missing receipts, failed cleanup or uncertain effects enter
reconciliation/quarantine, not success or blind retry.

BehaviorReceipt pins source/build/artifact/site/environment/subject/resource/
server/capability/schema/recipe/fixture/input/pre/post/rollback/generation, signer,
expiry and revocation. L4 eligibility refresh uses only matching current receipts
and existing grants within an already reviewed Staging projection. No new mount,
new permission, Production activation or Breakglass is automatic.

## Registry and pack lifecycle

CertificationPack separates engine code from policies, manifests, recipes,
compatibility evidence and Skill/profile templates. Require trusted signature/key
role, version/interpreter compatibility, exact applicability, immutable digest,
atomic activation, audit, expiry/revocation and anti-replay/restore protection.
Artifact change invalidates affected pack bindings until real new verification;
the old exact package receipt cannot certify a changed artifact.

Restrictive/equal non-authorizing refresh may be policy-admitted. Risk downgrade,
broader object/data scope, longer authority TTL, grants, new activation eligibility
or trust changes remain reviewed governance. Pack rollback cannot resurrect
consumed/revoked authority or older unsafe policy semantics. A signature is not
permission and a pack cannot manufacture behavioral or external evidence.

## Three-way reconciliation and update acceptance

OwnershipRecord links exact field/resource, managed baseline, human/provider
lineage and lock policy. ReconciliationPlan compares last-managed/current/desired
with CAS and minimal authorized delta. Proven managed non-security drift may be
repaired. Valid unlocked human changes are preserved/rebased. Missing lineage,
conflict, lock, security/authority field or concurrent change requires review.
No overwrite of edited Skills, automatic spend unfreeze/profile resume or initial
Brand/Production authority creation.

UpdateAcceptanceReceipt links exact pre/post artifacts/graphs/active workflows/
schemas/authority/topology/performance, semantic outcome, tests, isolated effects,
registry/catalog/Skill/grant projection and verification. No drift/read drift,
changed write, disappeared capability, changed reversibility, authority/topology
change and new side channel have independent outcomes. Safe repairs and release
rollback are distinct; rollback needs an exact preauthorized trusted recovery
plan with retained evidence/restore epochs. Unknown external effects never retry.

## Host and external providers

HostCapabilityObservation is measured, host/environment/provenance-bound and
expiring. Missing prlimit/network sandbox blocks Developer execution; remediation
is an external host action, not WordPress binary installation or unsandboxed
fallback. Generic process probing and arbitrary command execution are forbidden.

ExternalProviderDescriptor covers reviewed credential fields, account/property
discovery, scopes, quota/currency/economics/rates/rights/health, native capabilities,
effects and reconciliation. Shared UX/plans interpret admitted strategies, not
arbitrary vendor endpoint configuration. New Google scopes still require external
consent; initial Brand decisions and high-risk/Production authority remain owner
actions. Unknown protocol/cost/rights cannot become automatic paid execution.

## Operator and acceptance

Action Center states: AUTO_REPAIRED, APPROVAL_REQUIRED, EXTERNAL_ACTION_REQUIRED,
RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED. Every repaired outcome includes real plan/readback proof;
statuses cannot hide manual decisions, loops, conflicts or missing effects.
Journal projects existing execution/audit/approval/reversal evidence, with affected
objects, actor/agent/reason, before/after, provider/capability/authority, expiry,
external effects and honest Undo eligibility. It creates no second authority plane.

Required negatives include hostile discovered metadata, unsafe GET/read annotation,
arbitrary manifest binding, missing canary authority, wrong/expired/revoked packs,
human ownership conflict, partial update/rollback, stale workers, changed topology,
new side channel, external uncertainty and fabricated automation metrics. Runtime
and exact live/browser/provider evidence remain separate from Spec integrity.

## Resilience extension contracts

Fleet promotion binds exact site/cohort/generation identity and never copies grants or Production authority. Automatic L2–L4 actions consume retry/error budgets and obey an independent durable kill switch. Supply-chain signatures prove provenance only. Registry migrations are versioned, dry-run, reversible and cannot silently lower risk. Fuzzing is deterministic and disposable only. Restore creates a new epoch: stale mutable execution evidence is invalidated and current artifact/provider/host plus site-local authority must be reconciled before governed writes resume.
