---
name: wordpress-commerce-quote-booking-gates
description: Audit the separately authorized transitions from a verified nonbinding quote draft to manager-approved offer, traveler acceptance, WooCommerce order, supplier booking and operational fulfillment without bypassing MAD4B capability gates.
---

Use when planning or reviewing commercial conversion of a qualified inquiry or an existing nonfinancial Commerce Request. This Skill is a read-only orchestrator and does NOT introduce checkout, payment, booking or supplier-write abilities.

1. Resolve exact Site Profile/environment, source submission and qualified service/Rate evidence, Request Registry or equivalent owner, latest revision, existing tasks, quote provider identity, and product identity. Refuse stale, contradictory, unsupported or cross-site source pointers.
2. Separate these states: `request_received`, `under_review`, `qualified`, `quote_draft`, `quote_requires_review`, `quote_price_certified`, `manager_quote_approved`, `traveler_accepted`, `order_authorized`, `supplier_confirmed`, `fulfilled`. They are distinct business facts, not aliases for a provider's generic status.
3. `accepted_for_processing` is an operational review decision, NOT final price approval or customer acceptance. Do not promote a quote based on a manager role alone.
4. Require a current domain-owned final quote containing complete priced/unpriced lines, currency/FX evidence, date and rate windows, occupancy/age policy, hotels/transport/extras, availability status, taxes/fees, refund/deposit terms, supplier costs and financial policy as applicable. Any missing required component keeps `quote_requires_review`; never convert an unavailable price to zero.
5. A manager's commercial approval must be a separately authorized, auditable action bound to the exact quote revision/digest, financial terms, actor and provider readback. Operator permission to qualify or review cannot approve price, payments or suppliers.
6. Traveler acceptance is a distinct, authenticated or otherwise policy-certified first-party evidence event tied to the *same immutable quote digest*, terms, currency, total and expiry. Email opened, WhatsApp delivered, CRM contact linked or a Bit Flows notification is not acceptance.
7. Before WooCommerce order creation, require fresh quote approval and traveler acceptance plus the site's certified order-item mapping, product/Rate eligibility, payment/checkout policy, exact mutation grant, replay/idempotency guard and rollback/reconciliation. A nonfinancial `checkout-draft` or pending Woo order is not proof of a paid/confirmed sale.
8. Before supplier booking, independently certify supplier identity, availability/hold, order/payment dependencies, chosen service and Rate, deposit/refund terms, supplier confirmation contract, approval and exact idempotent booking write. Supplier timeout or ambiguous commit must stop for reconciliation, not blind retry.
9. Delegate reminders, notifications, delays and webhooks to the existing Workflow Queue/Worker and certified Bit Flows adapter as mechanics only. A signed webhook proves transport identity, not financial authorization or the traveler decision. Do not edit Bit Flows internals or provider databases.
10. Execute only separately mounted MAD4B abilities under current exact Site Profile, NHI grant, one-time approval, budget, plan digest, provider certification and postcondition readback. If any capability is absent or unmounted, stop at `BLOCKED / PLAN_ONLY`, not a simulated successful transition.
11. Audit each gate with before/after fingerprints, actor, quote revision, decision time, evidence owner, external provider response and allowed rollback or reconciliation path; redact PII and sensitive payment data. Use a distinct Production approval and do not reuse Local/Staging receipts.
12. Return a stage-by-stage readiness matrix showing verified, unknown and blocked gates and a single next safe owner action. Never report `Booking Confirmed` without a certified supplier confirmation receipt.
